A contact section that holds up on mobile

HTML · CSS Beginner 5 min read 26 Nov 2023
fig. 01 — the finished buildhtml · css · js

Address, phone and email on one side, a working form on the other.

Every site needs a way to be reached, and most of them get it wrong on a phone. This layout stacks the details on top and keeps the form thumb-sized instead of shrinking both columns.

What you'll build

  • Two-column panel that flips to one column under 820px
  • Labelled inputs with generous hit areas
  • Email and phone as real links
  • A divider that disappears when the columns stack

Files in the download

  • index.html
  • style.css

Before you start

You need a text editor and a browser — that's it. No package manager, no build step, no framework. Open index.html and the demo runs.

If you change something and it breaks, open the browser console first. Nine times out of ten the answer is a typo in a selector or a variable used before it exists.
